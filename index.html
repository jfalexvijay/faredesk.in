FareDesk — Invoice Generation App

We're here to help you use FareDesk with confidence. Find answers to common questions below. If you need further assistance, you can report a problem or share a suggestion.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I create an invoice?

  1. Open the Invoice tab.
  2. Tap Create in the top-right corner.
  3. Enter the required customer, invoice, item, fare, tax, payment, and other details.
  4. Review the information carefully.
  5. Tap the tick icon in the top-right corner to proceed to invoice PDF generation.

Make sure all amounts, tax details, and customer information are correct before generating the final invoice.

2. Can I save an invoice as a draft?

Yes. You can save invoice details as a draft and return to add or update invoice items before generating the final PDF.

3. Can I edit an invoice after generating the final PDF?

In the current version, an invoice cannot be edited through the app's workflow after the final invoice PDF has been generated. Please review all details carefully before completing the process.

4. How do I mark an invoice as paid?

  1. Open the Invoice tab.
  2. Select the Pending filter.
  3. Open the invoice you want to update.
  4. Tap the tick icon in the top-right corner.
  5. Select Payment Received on the confirmation screen.

Only mark an invoice as paid after you have verified that payment has been received.

5. Where are my invoices and PDF files stored?

FareDesk V1 stores invoice and business information locally on your device. Generated PDF files are also stored locally within the app and can be shared using the available options.

FareDesk V1 does not use CloudKit or iCloud syncing to back up or synchronize invoice records.

6. Will my data be available if I reinstall FareDesk?

Not necessarily. Uninstalling FareDesk may permanently remove locally stored business details, invoices, and PDF files that have not been saved elsewhere.

Before uninstalling the app or changing devices, save copies of important invoice PDFs to a secure location you control.

7. Can I use FareDesk without an internet connection?

Yes. The core invoice-generation features are designed to work offline and do not require an account. Some diagnostic services may need an internet connection.

8. How do I add my business logo?

Open the Business section and use the available logo option to select or capture your business logo. FareDesk may request photo library or camera access for this purpose.

If you do not add a logo, the default transparent FareDesk logo is used in the designated logo area of the generated invoice.

9. Can I add my bank account details?

Yes. You can enter your business bank account details in the Business section so they can appear on your generated invoice where supported.

Please verify these details before sharing an invoice.

10. Why should I review tax calculations?

Invoice calculations depend on the information and settings you enter. Always verify GST details, tax rates, discounts, totals, and other invoice information before issuing the invoice. Consult a qualified tax professional when necessary.

Report a Problem

If you experience an error or something does not work as expected, please report it so the issue can be investigated.

When reporting a problem, include:

  • What you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead.
  • The steps needed to reproduce the issue.
  • Any error message displayed.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The FareDesk app version.

Please do not include passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary personal, customer, or confidential business information in your report.

Use the Report a Problem option in the app, if available, to contact support.
