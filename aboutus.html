About FareDesk

Simple Invoicing for Small Businesses

FareDesk is an invoice-generation application developed by J. Alex Antony Vijay in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. It is designed to help small businesses create and manage invoices easily using their iPhone or iPad.

Our goal
To make everyday invoicing simple, convenient, and accessible, without unnecessary complexity.

What FareDesk Offers

  • Easy Invoice Creation: Create professional invoices with your business and customer details.
  • Business Information: Maintain your business details, including contact information, GST number, PAN, and bank account details.
  • Tax and Amount Calculations: Enter invoice items, charges, discounts, and applicable tax information.
  • PDF Invoices: Generate invoice PDFs that you can save and share.
  • Draft Management: Save invoice details as drafts and update them before generating the final invoice.
  • Offline Access: Use the core invoicing features without requiring an internet connection or creating an account.

Designed with Privacy in Mind

FareDesk V1 stores business and invoice information locally on your device. It does not use a FareDesk server or CloudKit to synchronize or back up your invoice records. You remain responsible for saving copies of important invoices and protecting the information stored on your device.

Our Vision

We aim to make practical business tools accessible to small businesses and independent entrepreneurs. We plan to improve FareDesk over time based on user needs and feedback.

Future development plans may include invoice reports and analysis, additional languages, cloud synchronization, and support for more platforms. These are plans for future development and may change.
