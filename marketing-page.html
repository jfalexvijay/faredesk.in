A simple workflow

Prepare an invoice in a few straightforward steps.

  1. Add your business information.
  2. Enter the customer details and invoice items.
  3. Review the invoice and save a draft if you need to finish it later.
  4. Generate a PDF and share it using your device's sharing options.
  5. Update and review payment status as needed.

Designed for iPhone and iPad

FareDesk is currently available for iPhone and iPad. The app is intended to make basic invoice preparation more convenient for small businesses.

Please note: FareDesk is an invoicing tool, not tax or accounting advice. You are responsible for checking invoice details and ensuring that your invoices comply with the rules applicable to your business.

Your invoices stay on your device

FareDesk's current version stores invoice information and generated PDFs locally in the app. It does not provide cloud synchronisation or server-based invoice storage.

Uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored information. Keep copies of important PDFs somewhere safe. Review the Privacy Policy for the full details of how the app handles information.
