Simple Invoice Generator · iPhone · iPad · Mac

Create invoices. Manage your business. Keep it simple.

FareDesk helps small businesses prepare invoices, save drafts, track payment status, add a business logo, and generate PDF invoices on Apple devices.

View FareDesk on the App Store

Everyday invoicing essentials

Invoice creation

Enter business, customer, and invoice-item details and review them before generating your invoice.

Draft management

Save invoice details as a draft and update them before generating the final PDF.

Payment status

Review invoice statuses such as pending, paid, and overdue.

PDF generation

Generate PDFs. Share them from iPhone or iPad, or save them to a chosen location on Mac.

Your business logo

Add your own logo so it appears in the generated invoice PDF.

Local storage

Invoice data stays in local app storage; cloud backup and synchronisation are not available in the current version.

Available on Apple devices

FareDesk supports iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with an interface adapted to each platform.

Important to know

No backup or data recovery in the current version.

If you delete or uninstall FareDesk, data stored inside the app may be permanently lost and cannot be recovered by FareDesk. Save or share important PDFs to a location you control. Exporting a PDF does not back up editable invoice records.

Reports, advanced filtering, and OTP or Face ID login are planned for a future version and are not currently available.
