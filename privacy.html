Privacy Policy

FareDesk — Invoice Generation App

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

FareDesk is an invoice-generation application developed by J. Alex Antony Vijay in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The app is designed to help small businesses create and manage invoices using their iPhone or iPad.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information FareDesk handles, how it is stored, and how it may be shared when you use the application.

2. Information We Store

FareDesk allows you to enter and manage business and invoice information, including:

  • Business name, address, contact details, email address, and website.
  • GST identification number, PAN, and business bank account details.
  • Customer information and invoice details.
  • Invoice items, prices, taxes, discounts, payment status, and due dates.
  • Business logo images selected or captured by you.

You choose what information to enter into the application. Please provide only information that is necessary for your business needs.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

FareDesk V1 stores invoice and business information locally on your device using the app's local storage.

FareDesk V1 does not use CloudKit, iCloud syncing, or a FareDesk server to store or synchronize your invoice data. The app does not automatically back up your invoice records to a cloud service.

Your generated invoice PDF files are stored locally within the app. You may choose to share or export these files using the sharing options available on your device. Once you share a file with another app or person, its handling is subject to that recipient's practices and policies.

FareDesk does not provide an automatic recovery service for locally stored data.

4. Internet Connection and Cloud Services

The main invoice-generation features of FareDesk are designed to work offline, without requiring an internet connection or a user account.

However, certain diagnostic services, such as crash reporting, may require an internet connection to transmit diagnostic information to their service provider.

FareDesk V1 does not use CloudKit to synchronize or back up your business or invoice information. Cloud-based features, if introduced in a future version, will be covered by an updated Privacy Policy where applicable.

5. Camera and Photo Library Access

FareDesk may request access to your camera or photo library when you choose to add a business logo.

This access is used for the logo feature. FareDesk does not need access to your photos for ordinary invoice creation.

If you do not add a business logo, the app uses its default transparent FareDesk logo in the designated logo area of the generated invoice.

6. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

FareDesk uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify application crashes and technical issues so that the app can be improved.

Crash reports may include technical information such as crash logs, app version, device information, operating system details, and diagnostic data associated with an error. Such information may be processed by Firebase in accordance with its own privacy terms.

FareDesk V1 does not use Google Analytics to track user activity.

Crash reporting is separate from invoice storage. FareDesk does not intentionally send your complete invoice database or generated invoice PDFs to Crashlytics as part of its ordinary crash-reporting functionality. Please avoid including personal or confidential invoice information in any diagnostic message you submit.

7. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

FareDesk does not operate a server that collects or stores your business and invoice records.

You may choose to share generated PDF invoices through other apps, services, or communication channels. When you do so, the information in those files may be received and processed by the services or people you select.

We do not sell your business or invoice information to third parties.

8. Data Security

FareDesk stores business and invoice information locally on your device. The app does not provide a separate, app-managed encryption or cloud-backup service for this information.

Your device's security settings and protections may help safeguard locally stored data, but no storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device protected and use caution when connecting it to computers or other devices you do not trust.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any invoice files you choose to export or share.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your business and invoice information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application and its associated local data.

Uninstalling FareDesk may permanently remove locally stored business information, invoices, and PDFs that have not been saved elsewhere. Reinstalling the application does not guarantee that previously stored data can be recovered.

If you need to retain an invoice, export or save its PDF to a location you control before uninstalling the application or changing devices.

10. Children's Privacy

FareDesk is intended for business use and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a FareDesk-operated server.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. Any revised version will be made available through an appropriate app or publication channel, together with an updated effective date.

If future versions introduce cloud synchronization, account-based features, or additional data processing, this policy will be reviewed and updated to describe those practices before or when they are introduced, as applicable.
